JARVIS V4 voice test checklist

1. Start backend:
   python -m uvicorn app.main:app --reload --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8000

2. Open:
   http://127.0.0.1:8000/

3. Log in.
4. Open AI Chat.
5. Click Start Voice JARVIS.
6. Allow microphone permission.
7. Say: Hey JARVIS
8. After JARVIS says it is listening, say:
   What is the status of my AI provider?
9. Confirm that the response appears in chat and is spoken aloud.
10. Click Stop Voice and confirm microphone recognition stops.

If browser speech recognition is unavailable, use Chrome/Edge desktop.
